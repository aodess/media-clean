FAQ

What does Media Clean remove?

The technical records stored inside photo and video files, including records about how a file was generated. Photos are rebuilt from pixels and videos are re-encoded.

What is the file size limit?

Up to 20 MB per file.

Does it hide AI content from Instagram or TikTok?

No. It cleans file metadata only. Platforms can still detect or label AI-generated content by other means, and their rules about disclosing AI content still apply. Label your content where it is required.
