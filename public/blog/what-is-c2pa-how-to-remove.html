What is C2PA (Content Credentials) and how to remove it from photos and videos

C2PA is a standard for a file’s “provenance passport”. A signed record is embedded in the photo or video: which app made it, how it was edited, whether AI was used. It is also called Content Credentials.

Who writes C2PA

  • Image and video generators, for example Adobe Firefly and OpenAI services.
  • Editors with AI features: generative fill, object removal.
  • Some cameras and phones.

Why it matters

  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok read C2PA and use it to add an AI label.
  • The record stays in the file even after renaming or forwarding it.

How to remove C2PA

  • Send the photo or video to @AIMediaclean_bot.
  • The bot deletes the C2PA (JUMBF) blocks plus EXIF, XMP and IPTC, and verifies the file structure.
  • Photos keep their size, H.264 videos keep the original quality.

What C2PA removal does not do

  • It does not remove visible watermarks or marks drawn into the image itself.
