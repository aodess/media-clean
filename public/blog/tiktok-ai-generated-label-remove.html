TikTok “AI-generated” label: where it comes from and how to remove it from your video

TikTok automatically adds an “AI-generated” label when it finds generation records in the file. It often shows up on videos you only touched up with AI: upscaling, background removal, an effect.

Why TikTok adds the label

  • Many AI tools and editors write Content Credentials (C2PA) into the video — a signed record of how the file was made.
  • TikTok reads these records on upload and turns the label on automatically.
  • Files can also carry software and device tags.

How to clean the video

  • Open @AIMediaclean_bot and send the clip as a file.
  • The bot deletes the AI metadata and returns a clean MP4.
  • H.264 video is not recompressed: frames match the original, same quality.

Good to know

  • Files up to 50 MB.
  • You can also send a TikTok video link — the bot downloads it in original quality.
  • If a clip is fully generated, TikTok’s rules may require you to disclose it manually.
