How to remove the “AI info” label on Instagram from photos and videos

You post a photo or a Reel and “AI info” (formerly “Made with AI”) appears under your name. It happens to AI-generated clips and also to real photos that were only touched up in an editor.

Where the label comes from

  • AI generators and editors with AI features write hidden records into the file: C2PA (Content Credentials), XMP, IPTC, the software name.
  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok read these records on upload and add the label automatically.
  • You can’t see this data — the file looks normal from the outside.

How to remove the label

  • Open @AIMediaclean_bot in Telegram and press Start.
  • Send a photo or video — preferably as a file, so Telegram doesn’t compress it.
  • A few seconds later you get a clean file without the AI metadata. Post it.

Good to know

  • No quality loss: photos keep their size, H.264 videos are not recompressed — frames match the original.
  • Files up to 50 MB, iPhone photos (HEIC) supported.
  • Tested: after cleaning, Instagram did not label a single one of our photos or videos.
  • The bot does not remove visible watermarks.
